Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

The short version: FilaTint doesn't collect anything. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The 3MF files you open and paint stay on your device, and we never see them.

Your files

FilaTint opens the 3MF files you choose through the iOS file picker and saves your exports wherever you choose. All importing, painting and exporting happens on your device. We don't run a server, and the app never uploads your models or project settings anywhere.

No account

You don't need to sign up or log in, and the app doesn't ask for your name, email or any other personal information.

Preferences

A few settings, such as whether edge outlines are shown, are saved locally on your device so they stick between launches. They're never sent to us.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, ad networks or analytics services. If you save files to iCloud Drive or another storage provider, that provider's own privacy policy applies, such as Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

FilaTint isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
